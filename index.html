Work

Automating a quality workflow

Tetra Pak, Supply Network Quality

One step in the quality workflow was manual and kept producing input errors. On my own initiative I built a script in Python and SAP GUI that removed it.

I tested it through several iterations before my colleagues started using it daily. It saves about 40 minutes a day, an estimated SEK 80,000 a year.

A KPI meant to change behavior

Tetra Pak, Supply Network Quality

I mapped the process flow and proposed a new KPI for operators, grounded in behavioral economics. The goal was to raise quality awareness rather than just track a metric.

The thinking owes a lot to Nudge by Thaler and Sunstein.

Costing a Formula Student car

Lund Formula Student, Cost & Manufacturing Lead

In a team of three, I was mainly responsible for the financial cost models of the 2025 car: 10 sub-assemblies, 460 process steps and 27 manufacturing types.

The team's Cost & Manufacturing score rose 20% from the year before. We ranked 10 of 44 at Formula Student Netherlands and 11 of 53 at Formula Student Czech.

Running a student union's treasury

LundaEkonomerna, Treasury Project Leader

I led a team of five handling daily finances, budget tracking and internal controls in Fortnox, for a student union with SEK 10 million in annual revenue.

I also started moving the internal documents from Swedish to English, to open the committee to international students.

About

I approach business with a technical interest and an economic perspective. I studied Business Administration with an International Business focus at Lund University, and spent an exchange semester at the National University of Singapore on data-driven decision-making and business analytics.
